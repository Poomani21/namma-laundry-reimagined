# Namma Laundry Reimagined

Namma Laundry — Website Redesign

Act as a 20+ years experienced senior UI/UX designer and web designer with strong experience in local service businesses, conversion-focused websites, Google Ads landing pages and SEO-friendly website architecture.

Completely redesign the Namma Laundry website into a premium, clean, modern, trustworthy and highly conversion-focused laundry-care website.

Brand & References

Website/domain: nammalaundry.in

Brand colours: Bottle Green + White + Golden

I will provide the Namma Laundry logo and reference images.

The reference images are our current WhatsApp/store offer designs. Use them as the visual reference for colour combinations, golden/bottle-green treatment, typography feel and brand identity, but do NOT simply copy the offer designs.

Create a professional website design based on those references.

Business Positioning

Position Namma Laundry as an experienced and trusted local laundry-care provider with around 10 years of experience.

The website should feel:

Premium

Clean

Professional

Trustworthy

Modern

Local

Easy to understand

Mobile-first

Do NOT make the website overly decorative, crowded or complicated.

Website Structure

Keep navigation extremely simple while supporting multiple pages:

Home → Services → Individual Service Pages → Pricing → Online Estimate → Knowledge Centre → About Us → Reviews → Contact/Booking

Important services should have their own landing pages, such as:

Blankets

Curtains

Carpets

Home Linen

Dry Cleaning

Sarees

Shoes

Bags

Wash & Fold

Wash & Iron

Steam Ironing

Stain Removal

Other relevant laundry services

The architecture must make it easy to add more services and knowledge articles later.

Online Estimate

Pricing must be an important part of the website.

Create an Online Estimate flow where customers can select services/items and see an estimated price.

The final CTA should naturally lead to:
Book Order / WhatsApp / Call

Design the system so pricing can eventually be managed from a backend/admin panel.

Knowledge Centre

Create a useful Laundry & Fabric Care Knowledge Centre, covering topics such as:

Blanket cleaning frequency

Curtain cleaning frequency

Carpet and upholstery cleaning

Seasonal/monsoon care

Pet households

Homes with children or elderly people

Dust/allergen-prone homes

Fabric-care tips

Washing vs dry cleaning vs specialised cleaning

When professional cleaning is recommended

Content should be short, useful, credible and customer-focused, not generic AI-generated filler.

Trust & Conversion

Clearly highlight:

10+ years of experience

Local service presence

Customer reviews

Professional cleaning process

Service areas

Before/after images where suitable

Strong customer testimonials

Clear pricing

Easy booking/contact

Use strong CTAs throughout the website without making them repetitive.

Content Style

Use:
Strong content + Simple language + Easy navigation + Useful information + Professional design + Easy conversion

A customer should quickly understand:

What we do → Why they need it → How often they need it → How much it costs → Why choose us → How to order

Avoid long paragraphs and unnecessary marketing language.

Google Ads & SEO

Build the design and page structure with Google Ads and SEO readiness in mind.

Each major service should be capable of becoming a dedicated landing page with:

Clear service-specific headline

Benefits

Pricing/estimate CTA

Trust signals

FAQs

Reviews

WhatsApp/Call/Booking CTA

Responsive Design

Design and test carefully for:
Desktop, tablet and mobile, especially mobile because Google Ads traffic will be significant.

Important Design Rule

This is a simple local-business website, not a huge corporate website.

Keep the design elegant and focused. Use whitespace, strong typography, premium bottle-green sections, subtle golden highlights, clean cards and professional imagery.

Do not add unnecessary animations or complicated UI.

First priority: excellent website architecture, user journey and visual design. Then build the pages and functionality around that design.

Use the uploaded logo and reference images throughout the design wherever appropriate. Do not replace the brand identity with a generic laundry template.
